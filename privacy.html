Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how it stays yours.

1. Information we collect 2. How we use it 3. Sharing 4. Cookies 5. Data security 6. Your rights 7. Data retention 8. Contact
Last updated: September 2026

1. Information we collect

To build and run your program, we collect:

  • Account details — name, email, and payment information.
  • Intake information — your goals, schedule, food preferences, and activity level.
  • Progress data — check-in responses, messages with your coach, and plan history.
  • Usage data — device and browser information collected automatically when you use our site or app.

2. How we use it

We use your information to build and personalize your nutrition and movement plan, match and support your coach, process billing, and improve the program based on aggregate, de-identified trends. We do not use your health information for advertising.

3. Sharing

We share information only with the coach assigned to you, payment processors needed to bill your plan, and service providers who help us run the platform under confidentiality agreements. We do not sell your personal information.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and remember your preferences, and analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

5. Data security

We encrypt data in transit and at rest, and limit internal access to your health and program information to the coach and support staff working with you.

6. Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct inaccurate information, or request deletion of your account at any time from your settings or by contacting us directly.

7. Data retention

We keep your account and program data for as long as your account is active, and for a limited period afterward to comply with legal and billing obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@halcyonwellness.com or visit our contact page.
